# Word lists

`tr.txt` and `en.txt` are derived from [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by Hermit Dave, built from OpenSubtitles. `tr.txt` keeps words seen at least 10 times; `en.txt` is the 50k list.
